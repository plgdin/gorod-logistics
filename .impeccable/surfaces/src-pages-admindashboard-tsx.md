# Surface Brief: Operations Dashboard & Customs Ledger (`/admin`)

- **Scope**: `src/pages/AdminDashboard.tsx`
- **Visitor Mode**: `operate`
- **Audience**: Warehouse Keeper, Customs Bond Officer, Warehouse Manager, Accounts Desk, Security Gate Staff.
- **Task**: Inspect active consignments, enforce 1:1 BOE ratio, verify weighbridge delta within statutory ±5 kg tolerance, clear Accounts NDC, and maintain Customs compliance logs under Indian Customs Act 1962.
- **Tone**: Clean Enterprise Light Desk — crisp white & stone ground, high legibility under bright warehouse lighting, high tabular density, clear regulatory authority.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A high-integrity statutory customs and bonded warehouse operations ledger that completely refuses the generic dark-mode AI SaaS template (no glowing gradients, no neon pills, no hero stat cards). The page treats every consignment as a regulated legal custody record with crisp tabular density and zero decorative slop.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm institutional slate-white background (`#f8fafc` / `#ffffff`) with hairline borders (`#e2e8f0` / `#cbd5e1`), Gorod petroleum navy accents (`#0c323b`), customs crimson accents (`#852929`), and sharp emerald/amber regulatory state chips. Strict tabular figures, compact status pills, monospaced cargo identifiers, and authentic customs stamp typography.

### STORY
The warehouse keeper or compliance officer instantly confirms the station's legal authority (CBW TRV1U001, Indian Customs Act 1962 Sec 57/58), scans live consignment flows, spots weighbridge breaches (> ±5 kg) requiring customs escalation, and triggers role-specific statutory actions with immediate feedback.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
An authoritative station header with facility license, active officer badge, and direct role switch; an integrated Customs Metric Strip displaying live job counts, active holds, NDC clearances, and database synchronization in a clean inline ledger; a segmented operational tab bar and search filter; and an expansive high-density consignment master table with sticky header and inline role action triggers. Primary action: 'Register Inward BOE (SOP 4.1.1)' positioned prominently in the station header.
