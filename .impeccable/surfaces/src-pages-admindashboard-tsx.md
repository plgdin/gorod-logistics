# Surface Brief: Operations Dashboard & Customs Ledger (`/admin`)

- **Scope**: `src/pages/AdminDashboard.tsx`
- **Visitor Mode**: `operate`
- **Audience**: Warehouse Keeper, Customs Bond Officer, Warehouse Manager, Accounts Desk, Security Gate Staff.
- **Task**: Inspect active consignments, enforce 1:1 BOE ratio, verify weighbridge delta within statutory ±5 kg tolerance, clear Accounts NDC, and maintain Customs compliance logs under Indian Customs Act 1962.
- **Tone**: Clean Enterprise Light Desk — crisp white & stone ground, high legibility under bright warehouse lighting, high tabular density, clear regulatory authority.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A high-integrity statutory customs and bonded warehouse operations ledger that completely refuses the generic dark-mode AI SaaS template and floating-card slop (no hero banners, no fake checklist cards, no disconnected stat boxes, no row-height jumping). The page is an integrated, high-density operational workbench designed for instant comprehension and rapid regulatory decision-making.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm institutional slate-white background (`#f8fafc` / `#ffffff`) with hairline borders (`#e2e8f0`), Gorod petroleum navy accents (`#0c323b`), customs crimson accents (`#852929`), and sharp emerald/amber regulatory state chips. Strict tabular figures, compact status pills, monospaced cargo identifiers, and authentic customs stamp typography.

### STORY
The warehouse keeper or compliance officer instantly confirms station authority (CBW TRV1U001, Indian Customs Act 1962 Sec 57/58), immediately notices any active statutory holds (> ±5 kg) via a dedicated action banner, filters consignments via an integrated tab bar with live counters, and inspects deep custody/weighbridge records via a slide-over drawer without losing table context.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
An authoritative station header with facility license, active officer badge, and direct role switch; a streamlined consignment operations toolbar with direct actions ('Register Inward BOE' and 'SOP Manual'); a contextual statutory hold alert banner; a unified master data grid with integrated tab filters, live count badges, and quick search; and a slide-over inspection drawer for multi-step regulatory review.
